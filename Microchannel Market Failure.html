Infant Mortality & Early Failure Trajectory

Failures trough in the 3rd Quarter (14.8%) during off-peak seasonal usage before rising in the 4th Quarter (22.6%) as units approach their first annual service inspection and pre-warranty cutoff.

Barcode Architecture & Protocol Compliance

ODU chassis types are identified from the standard 16-character barcode structure (^[0-9]{2}X([A-Za-z])[0-9]{4}), where the letter immediately following capacity denotes ODU chassis. M and F chassis are normalized into unified M/F ODU (M: 21, F: 85). All 195 legacy records bearing prefix BXX0103... (WSI-RIVERINE-24C & WSI-VENTURI-24C 2.0 Ton) are verified C ODU chassis and fully incorporated into C ODU (2,942 records, 57.86%), achieving 100% population resolution.

Rigorous Spare-Parts Evidence Standard

Zero cross-field inference was applied. Condenser Type, Model name, Customer comments, and Expert Found Problems were not used to assign coated status. Even when other components mention "Black" or "Coatec" (e.g. black vane guides or coated evaporators), only entries with the exact phrase Color-Black For Coatec Series (parts SPAR.AIRC.446435 & 446436) are classified as Coated. All 370 coated events occurred in 2024 (46), 2025 (30), and 2026 (294).
