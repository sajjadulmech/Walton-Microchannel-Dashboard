Infant Mortality & Early Failure Trajectory

Within the first operating year, failure concentration is heaviest in the 1st Quarter (0–<3 months: 31.7%, 700 records) driven by shipping stress and factory joint micro-leaks. Failures ease in the 2nd (21.1%, 466 records) and 3rd (18.5%, 408 records) quarters before rising in the 4th Quarter (28.7%, 633 records) ahead of the 1-year annual warranty inspection.

Fleet Workhorse Engineering Insights

The 1.5 Ton capacity (18,000 BTU/hr) represents Walton's core residential and commercial fleet workhorse, bearing nearly 60% of all failure complaints. U-bend joint leakage constitutes the dominant mode across all tonnages (65.4% in 1.5 Ton, 60.2% in 2.0 Ton, and 63.9% in 1.0 Ton), showing uniform thermal-mechanical vulnerability.
